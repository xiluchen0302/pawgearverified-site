How our links work

How We Make Money

PawGearVerified is reader-supported. Commercial links help fund our work without adding a cost to you.

PawGearVerified may earn a commission when you buy through links on our website, at no additional cost to you.

How affiliate support works

Affiliate income helps fund product research, testing, photography, hosting, and ongoing site maintenance. A commission is paid by the retailer or affiliate network, not by the reader.

Editorial independence

Affiliate compensation does not buy a recommendation, a higher rating, or more favorable placement. We make editorial decisions independently and recommend products only when our testing and research support the recommendation. Commercial relationships do not override what we observe, what we can verify, or the limitations readers should know about.

Links and disclosure

We participate in affiliate programs operated by retailers and affiliate networks, and we may link to third-party sellers. We label affiliate links or place a clear disclosure where they appear. If a page contains affiliate links, assume that PawGearVerified may receive compensation from a qualifying purchase.

Product information can change

Prices, availability, features, and seller policies can change. Confirm the latest information with the seller before buying. A recommendation is not a guarantee that a product will suit every pet or household; proper fit, supervision, and use matter.
